RitualIQ Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

RitualIQ ("we," "our," or "us") is a skincare routine tracker mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create a RitualIQ account, we collect your email address and a securely hashed password. Authentication is handled by our backend provider, Supabase.

Profile Information

To personalize your experience, we collect your skin type and skincare concerns (e.g. acne, dryness, redness) that you choose to share with us.

Content You Create

  • Shelf products: names, brands, categories, ingredients, and optional buy links you add.
  • Routines: the AM/PM steps you build from your shelf products.
  • Check-ins and streaks: dates you mark a routine as completed.
  • Progress photos: photos you choose to upload to track changes over time, stored in a private, access-controlled location.
  • Skin Coach chat history: messages (and optional photos) you send to our AI Skin Coach feature, along with the AI's replies.

Camera and Photo Library

With your permission, RitualIQ accesses your device's camera and photo library to let you take or choose progress photos, scan a product barcode, and photograph a product for AI analysis (the Product Scanner feature). We only access these when you actively use these features.

Notifications

Refill reminders and routine reminders are scheduled locally on your device. We do not operate a push notification server, and no data leaves your device for this feature.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and personalize the app's core features (routines, ingredient conflict checks, skin-match scoring, refill predictions).
  • Power the AI Skin Coach and Product Scanner features, which send the relevant parts of your request (your message, a photo if attached, and relevant profile/shelf context) to our AI provider to generate a response.
  • Show you advertising, including personalized ads where you've given permission (see Section 3).
  • Process one-time purchases (e.g. removing ads).
  • Communicate with you about your account when necessary.
  • Maintain the security and integrity of the app.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Third-Party Services We Use

RitualIQ relies on the following third-party services, each of which processes a limited, relevant slice of your data:

  • Supabase — our backend provider for authentication, database, and file storage. Your account, profile, and content data are stored on Supabase's infrastructure.
  • Anthropic (Claude AI) — powers the AI Skin Coach chat and Product Scanner. When you use these features, your message text and any photo you attach, along with relevant context from your profile and shelf, are sent to Anthropic's API to generate a response. See Anthropic's own privacy policy at anthropic.com/privacy.
  • Open Beauty Facts / UPCitemdb — used to look up product information when you scan a barcode. Only the barcode number is sent; no personal information is shared with these services.
  • Google AdMob — displays advertising within the app. On iOS, AdMob may request permission to track your activity across other apps and websites for personalized advertising, which you can allow or decline via Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can also remove ads entirely via a one-time in-app purchase.
  • RevenueCat — manages and validates in-app purchases (such as removing ads). RevenueCat receives a purchase identifier tied to your account to track entitlement status.
  • Amazon Associates — some links in the app (e.g. "buy refill" links and featured product cards) are affiliate links to Amazon. Tapping one opens Amazon's site in your browser, where Amazon's own privacy policy applies. We may earn a commission on qualifying purchases.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We share information only as described above with our service providers, or if required to by law, to protect our rights, or in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets (in which case we will notify you).

5. Data Retention

We retain your account and content data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your profile, products, routines, check-ins, progress photos, and chat history are permanently deleted (see Section 6).

6. Your Rights and Choices

  • Access and update: you can view and edit your profile, products, routines, and reminders directly within the app.
  • Delete your account: you can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from Settings → Delete account within the app. This action is immediate and cannot be undone.
  • Camera, photo, and tracking permissions: you can grant or revoke camera, photo library, and (on iOS) App Tracking Transparency permissions at any time in your device's system settings.
  • Notifications: you can disable refill or routine reminders in Settings.

7. Data Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), access-controlled storage for photos, and row-level security on our database so that only you can access your own data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

RitualIQ is not directed at children under 13, or, in the EU/EEA, under the age of digital consent in their country (15 in France). We do not knowingly collect personal information from these children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. International Data Transfers

Our service providers (including Supabase, Anthropic, Google and RevenueCat) may process and store information in the United States or other countries outside the EU/EEA. When they do, the transfer relies on appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the "Last updated" date above when we do. Continued use of RitualIQ after changes means you accept the updated policy.

11. Your Rights Under the GDPR (EU/EEA Users)

If you are in the European Union or European Economic Area, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies to your data.

Legal Bases for Processing

  • Performance of a contract: your account, profile, content, routines, AI features you request, and purchases.
  • Consent: personalized advertising and tracking, camera and photo library access, and the skin concerns you choose to enter. You can withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier processing.
  • Legitimate interests: app security, fraud prevention, and non-personalized advertising.
  • Legal obligation: where the law requires us to keep or disclose information.

Skin concerns may reveal information about your health. Providing them is optional, and you can remove them from your profile at any time.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or export your data (data portability), and to object to processing based on legitimate interests. To exercise any of these rights, email us at the address below. We will respond within one month.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority. In France, this is the CNIL (www.cnil.fr).
