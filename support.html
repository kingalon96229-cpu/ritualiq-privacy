Frequently Asked Questions

How do I delete my account?
Open Settings → Delete account in the app. This permanently deletes your account and all of your data.

How do I remove ads?
Use the one-time "Remove Ads" purchase in the app. If you reinstall the app or change devices, use "Restore Purchases" in Settings.

How do I turn off reminders?
Open Settings in the app and switch off routine or refill reminders.

Is the AI Skin Coach medical advice?
No. RitualIQ offers general skincare information and is not a substitute for advice from a dermatologist or doctor.
